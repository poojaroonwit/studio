# User Personalization

## The Story of Your Workspace

| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **What** | Interface tools to tailor the HRI experience to your personal working style. |
| **Who** | **All Users** (Recruiters, Managers, Admins). |
| **When** | During initial onboarding or whenever your workflow needs optimization. |
| **Why** | To increase productivity by surface-level UI adjustments that matter to the individual. |
| **Where** | Accessed via **Profile Icon > Settings**. |
| **How** | 1. Go to **Settings** <br> 2. Change **Personal Color** <br> 3. Toggle **Dark Mode** <br> 4. Adjust applicant list density <br> 5. Set default Filters for your role |

## 1. Edit My Profile
Keep your account secure and recognizable.
1.  Click on your **Avatar icon** (top right) > **Profile**.
2.  **Profile Image**: Click the "Camera" icon to upload a photo (JPG/PNG). This helps your team recognize you in comments.
3.  **Password Change**:
    *   Enter current password.
    *   Enter new strong password (min 8 chars).
    *   Click **"Update Security"**.
4.  **Notifications**: Toggle email alerts for "New Applications" or "Mentioned in Comments".

## 2. Notification Center
Stay updated without email clutter.
1.  **Bell Icon**: Located in the top-right header.
    *   **Red Dot**: Indicates unread alerts.
    *   **Click**: Opens a dropdown of recent activities (e.g., "John Doe applied").
    *   **Mark All Read**: Clears the badge count.
2.  **Email Preferences**: Toggle which events send an email to your inbox vs just an in-app alert.

## 3. Visual Identity & Themes
- **Personal Color**: Change the application's accent color (buttons, highlights) to make your workspace feel unique.
- **Dark Mode**: Toggle between Light and Dark modes to reduce eye strain.

## 2. Workspace Optimization
- **Default View**: Choose whether you prefer seeing applicants in a **Kanban** board or a structured **Table** by default.
- **Sidebar Focus**: Toggle visibility of your "Assigned Positions" in the sidebar for one-click access to your most important roles.
- **Data Density**: Adjust how many rows of data you see per page to balance clarity with information depth.

## 3. How to Verify (Test Case)
To verify interface settings:
1.  **Navigate**: Go to your **Profile Settings**.
2.  **Act**: Toggle **"Dark Mode"** and change your **"Theme Color"**.
3.  **Confirm**: The application should instantly update its appearance. Refresh the page to ensure these preferences are saved to your user profile.
