# Mobile Access (PWA)

## The Story of Portable Recruitment

| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **What** | A full-featured mobile experience that mimics a native app without an app store download. |
| **Who** | **On-the-go Recruiters** and **Busy Executives** needing quick approvals. |
| **When** | During travel, between meetings, or while conducting face-to-face interviews. |
| **Why** | To eliminate delays in the recruitment cycle caused by being away from a desktop. |
| **Where** | Accessible via any modern smartphone browser (iOS/Android). |
| **How** | 1. Open URL in mobile browser <br> 2. Tap **"Share/Menu"** <br> 3. Select **"Add to Home Screen"** <br> 4. Launch from Home Screen <br> 5. Verify App-like experience |

## 1. Mobile-Optimized Interactions
The mobile app focuses on speed:
- **Card-UI**: Swipe through resumes with ease.
- **Quick Approvals**: Approve job requisitions or offers with a single tap.
- **Interview Mode**: A simplified interface for submitting star ratings during an interview.

## 2. Setting up the PWA
For the best experience, install HRI as a home screen icon:
1. Open the platform URL in **Safari (iOS)** or **Chrome (Android)**.
2. Tap the **"Share"** or **"Menu"** button.
3. Select **"Add to Home Screen"**.

## 3. How to Verify (Test Case)
To test the PWA:
1.  **Navigate**: Open the platform on your mobile device.
2.  **Act**: Follow the "Add to Home Screen" steps and then close your browser.
3.  **Confirm**: Tap the new icon on your home screen. The app should launch in full-screen mode without the browser's address bar, providing a native-app feel.
