# Hiring Manager Guide: Talent Acquisition for Leaders

## The Story of Strategic Selection

| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **What** | A leadership toolkit for reviewing talent, providing feedback, and managing department headcount. |
| **Who** | **Department Heads**, **Hiring Managers**, and **Team Interviewers**. |
| **When** | When opening new requisitions or reviewing applicants pre-selected by the recruitment team. |
| **Why** | To give leaders direct visibility into their talent pipeline without administrative friction. |
| **Where** | The **Home** dashboard and the **applicants** review module. |
| **How** | 1. Monitor **Dashboard** tasks <br> 2. Refine **Job Requirements** <br> 3. Review **Shortlisted** applicants <br> 4. Record interview **Scorecards** <br> 5. Discuss applicants via **Comments** |

## How to Verify (E2E Test)
To verify the manager experience:
1.  **Dashboard**: Check your **"My Tasks"** widget for a pending scorecard.
2.  **Evaluate**: Click the task, fill in the star ratings, and submit.
3.  **Confirm**: Ensure the applicant's profile now shows your recommendations and that the task has cleared from your dashboard.
