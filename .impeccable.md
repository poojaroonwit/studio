## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are HR recruiters working in a hiring workflow. They use the product to find candidates, compare them, read resumes, and manage each candidate through recruiting stages.

### Brand Personality
The interface should feel business-oriented, AI-assisted, and friendly. It should support fast decisions without feeling cold or overly technical.

### Aesthetic Direction
Prioritize light mode first, while supporting both light and dark themes. The product should feel approachable for recruiting teams and should explicitly avoid looking like an engineer-focused tool or developer dashboard.

### Design Principles
1. Keep recruiting workflows scannable and decision-focused.
2. Make AI assistance feel practical and supportive, not flashy.
3. Favor approachable business UI over technical or developer-heavy aesthetics.
4. Preserve clarity and hierarchy across dense candidate management screens.
5. Design light mode as the primary experience, with dark mode as a complete secondary theme.
