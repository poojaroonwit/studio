# Candidate Review

## The Story of Shortlisting

| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **What** | The process of vetting candidates that have been pre-screened by Recruiters and AI. |
| **Who** | **Hiring Managers** who own the specific job requisition. |
| **When** | Whenever a Recruiter moves a candidate to the "Shortlisted" stage for your review. |
| **Why** | To bridge the gap between AI scoring and human expertise, ensuring only the most qualified candidates reach the interview stage. |
| **Where** | In the **Candidates** list, often filtered by **Status: Shortlisted**. |
| **How** | 1. Open Candidate Profile <br> 2. Review **Fit Score** & **Resume** <br> 3. Check **Timeline** notes <br> 4. Add comment for Recruiter <br> 5. (Optional) Use **"Compare"** to see side-by-side |

## 1. Reviewing Profiles
Recruiters will "Shortlist" candidates for your review. When reviewing a profile:

- **Fit Score**: Check the AI-generated score (0-100) for a quick baseline on how well they match the JD.
- **Resume**: Click the **"Files"** tab to view the original PDF/DOCX resume without leaving the app.
- **Timeline**: See the recruiter's initial screening notes and any automated test results.

## 2. Decision Making
You generally don't move candidates on the board yourself to preserve historical data. Instead, use the **Comments** section to instruct the recruiter:
- *"Looks good, let's interview."*
- *"Pass, missing React experience - AI overscored the legacy experience."*

## 3. How to Verify (Test Case)
To verify you can effectively review candidates:
1.  **Navigate**: Click on **"Applicants"** in the sidebar.
2.  **Act**: Select a candidate in the **"Shortlisted"** stage.
3.  **Confirm**: Ensure the **Resume viewer** loads correctly in the "Files" tab and that you can type a comment in the timeline. Your comment should appear instantly for the Recruiter to see.
